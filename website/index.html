COW PROTOCOL / BUILT INTO DEKXDIS

How DEKXDIS powers your trades
for the lowest fees.

Let the market compete for your trade. DEKXDIS integrates CoW Protocol directly, putting a network of independent solvers to work finding you the best available price.

  1. 01

    Your order joins a batch

    Your signed order goes straight from DEKXDIS to a CoW batch auction, where it is grouped with other trades.

  2. 02

    Solvers compete for your trade

    Independent solvers search across liquidity sources and compete to find the best execution for the batch.

  3. 03

    Match directly. Skip pool fees.

    When trades match peer-to-peer, they bypass liquidity pools and their fees. Remaining trades use the best available liquidity.

MEV protection built in

CoW’s batch auctions protect your orders from front-running and sandwich attacks. Your signed price limit sets the terms of execution.

Swap gas, paid in your sell token

Network costs come from the token you sell. There is no swap gas charge for failed, unfilled or cancelled signed orders.

What you pay / CoW Protocol & DEKXDIS fees+

Low volume fees

0.02% for standard assets.
0.003% for correlated assets, such as stablecoins.

CoW fee details ↗

A share of better execution

CoW takes 50% of surplus above your limit on out-of-market limit orders, or 50% of positive quote improvement on marketable orders. Each is capped at 0.98% of the order’s volume.

DEKXDIS’s fee

0.05% per executed CoW trade — $0.50 per $1,000 traded — in addition to CoW fees and network costs. Free to download, with no subscription.
